Privacy Policy

Application: CloudSave Sync • Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CloudSave Sync handles information when you connect your Google Account to synchronize local game saves and backup folders with your own Google Drive.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize CloudSave Sync via Google OAuth 2.0, the application only requests the Google API permissions needed to operate your personal save-sync workflow:

  • Google Drive File & Folder Access: Used strictly to read, upload, update, and restore the save files and backup folders you select for synchronization in your own Google Drive.
  • Basic Account Identification (if applicable): Used only to display the connected account status locally in your sync client.

2. How Your Data Is Used and Stored

  • Direct Device-to-Drive Transfer: Files are transferred directly between your device and Google Drive servers over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Local Credential Storage: OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on your own device/configuration storage so the sync client can authenticate with Google Drive on your behalf.
  • No Central Developer Servers: CloudSave Sync does not operate any external tracking, analytics, or file-relay servers. The developer cannot view, access, or collect the contents of your Google Drive or local save files.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

CloudSave Sync does not sell, rent, trade, or share your Google user data or file contents with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure):
CloudSave Sync’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention and Revoking Access

Your synced backup files remain in your own Google Drive under your sole control until you choose to delete them. You can stop using CloudSave Sync and remove all stored data at any time by:

  • Deleting the local OAuth token or configuration file from your device.
  • Revoking CloudSave Sync’s access directly from your Google Account permissions page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Security

All communications with Google OAuth and Google Drive APIs use standard TLS/HTTPS encryption. You are responsible for safeguarding the device and local configuration files where your OAuth tokens are stored.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
