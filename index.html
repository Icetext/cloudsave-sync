How CloudSave Sync Works

Direct Google Drive Storage

Save files and folder backups are uploaded directly from your device to your own Google Drive storage. No third-party cloud servers sit in the middle.

User-Controlled OAuth Access

CloudSave Sync uses standard Google OAuth 2.0 authorization so you stay in full control and can inspect or revoke access from your Google Account at any time.

Zero Data Monetization

We do not collect, sell, analyze, or share your files or personal data. OAuth credentials stay local to your sync environment.

Google API & User Data Transparency

CloudSave Sync requests Google Drive permissions solely to upload, download, list, and synchronize save-game directories and local backup folders that you explicitly configure. Authentication tokens remain on your own machine and are never transmitted to any external developer server.

For full details on data handling, Limited Use compliance, and revocation instructions, please review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
